
# Project: Personal Developer Blog with Arabic/English Support

I want to build a personal blog where I can publish articles about software engineering, learning, and my experiences as a developer.

## Core problem

Arabic and English text can become difficult to read when mixed in the same paragraph. English technical terms, such as `.NET`, `React`, URLs, numbers, and punctuation, may appear in confusing positions when the text direction is incorrect.

I want the blog to provide a reliable reading and writing experience for Arabic, English, and mixed-language content.

## Initial goals

- Support Arabic RTL and English LTR layouts.
- Handle mixed Arabic/English paragraphs correctly.
- Display code blocks, inline code, URLs, numbers, and punctuation without breaking the reading experience.
- Provide readable typography for both Arabic and English.
- Include a writing experience with a preview of the published article.
- Support responsive layouts on mobile and desktop.
- Keep the initial scope small enough for a personal MVP.

## Your task

Use the project's existing instructions and repository context, if available, before making architectural decisions.

Interview me about the product requirements, user experience, technical constraints, content model, and publishing workflow.

Challenge assumptions and identify edge cases. Do not invent decisions on my behalf. Record unresolved questions explicitly.

For this phase, focus on understanding and documenting the project. Do not implement the application or generate implementation tickets until the requirements are sufficiently clear.
